About Me

I studied Computer Science, so I understand how things work behind the scenes. But most of what I know came from just doing it - building, breaking, fixing, and building again for years. That mix is what makes my work solid on the inside and beautiful on the outside.

I work with pretty much everything the web throws at me - from code that makes things move (React, GSAP, Three.js) to platforms people already know, like Shopify and WordPress. Whether it's a simple site or a full 3D experience, I know how to build it, and build it well.

When I'm not coding, I focus on refining digital experiences, exploring creative web interactions, and continuous learning. I believe great products come from blending technical craft with thoughtful design intuition.
